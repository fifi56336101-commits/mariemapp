Wound Analysis

1

Patient Information

Enter clinical data for accurate analysis:

🎂 Age: Patient age in years
⚠️ Risk Factors: Diabetes, mobility issues
📍 Location: Anatomical site
⏱️ Duration: Time since onset
😣 Pain Level: Scale 1-10
🦠 Infection Signs: Redness, heat, pus
2

Capture Image

Take a clear photo of the wound:

  1. Ensure good lighting
  2. Position camera directly above
  3. Include surrounding skin
  4. Keep camera steady
💡 Pro Tip: Use consistent lighting and angles for better tracking over time.
3

Analysis Results

The AI provides comprehensive analysis:

  • Urgency: CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW
  • Stage: 1-4, Unstageable, DTI
  • Description: Wound characteristics
  • Infection Risk: Low, Medium, High
  • Recommendations: Care instructions
⚠️
Medical Disclaimer

This AI analysis is for educational purposes only. Always consult healthcare professionals for clinical decisions.
